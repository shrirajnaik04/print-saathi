# Photo gap, text messages, scanning pages — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Photos 10 mm apart; print a WhatsApp text message (share or paste); scan paper pages with the phone's camera.

**Architecture:**
- **Text:** it enters as a generated PDF item (`type: 'pdf'`, `text: {size, source}`), made by the isolated engine from the `.txt`, so the whole document pipeline is reused.
- **Scanned pages:** each is a photo item with four corners (`job.quads`) in a document job, straightened by the engine's existing quad warp.
- **New engine functions:** `textToPdf` and `detectPage`, plus a new style, `clean` (Clean colour).
- **Camera:** the phone's camera app writes through a private, non-exported provider straight into the job folder.

**Tech Stack:** Preact + TS, Kotlin (Capacitor plugin, isolated engine via AIDL), Vitest, Playwright, JUnit, AndroidX instrumentation, adb smokes.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-26-round-8-photo-gap-text-scan-design.md`

## Global Constraints
- Customer content is parsed only in the isolated engine. Text and photos live only under `noBackupFilesDir/jobs/`, and are never logged.
- No new dependency, no permission (no CAMERA), no network. Exact versions unchanged.
- Start-up JS ≤ 61,440 B gzip; new screens are lazy.
- Plain English, controls ≥ 56 px, every drag has a button.

## Review Focus
1. **Text with WhatsApp marks inside words or unbalanced** (`2*3*4`, `snake_case_name`, a lone `*`): printed as typed, never eaten.
2. **Very long or empty text, emoji and Devanagari mixed:** clear refusal or correct pages, never a crash in the engine.
3. **Camera returns nothing, the app is killed while the camera is open, or no camera app exists:** no lost photo, no stuck screen, no empty page.
4. **A page that can't be found** (blank photo, page filling the whole photo): the whole photo is proposed and the corners stay movable by buttons.
5. **Retake, Delete and reorder of scanned pages while one is open on Ready:** the plan and print follow; Undo restores.
