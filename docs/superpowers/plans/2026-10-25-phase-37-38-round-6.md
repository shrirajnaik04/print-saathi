# Undo / Redo and Bring to front / Send to back — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Word-like Undo / Redo for every change on Adjust, and Bring to front / Send to back for overlapping photos and ID cards.

**Architecture:**
- A per-job step store, kept only while the job's revision is the one the last step left, holds snapshots of the job's adjustable choices.
- Undo and Redo save a snapshot back through the single writer as one `restoreEdits` change.
- Layers are new reducer actions over the existing spots.

**Tech Stack:** Preact + TypeScript, Vitest, Playwright, adb device smoke.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-round-6-undo-redo-front-back-design.md`

## Global Constraints
- Web-app only: no Android, engine or PrintPlan contract change.
- Startup JS ≤ 61,440 B gzip (`npm run size`); Adjust stays lazy.
- Controls ≥ 56 px, accessible names; plain words: "Undo", "Redo", "Bring to front", "Send to back".
- At most 50 steps per job.
- Exact dependency versions; no new dependency.

## Review Focus
1. **An Undo right after a change made outside Adjust** (Change → Layout, a print, Add photos from Received): it must never take back that change. The steps are forgotten when the revision moved.
2. **Undo while a save is still running** (quick double taps): exactly one step undone. The buttons are disabled while busy, as every tool is.
3. **Undo of Remove or Cut while round 5's Undo panel or clipboard holds the same photo:** never restored twice, and Paste is never left pointing at a photo that is back.
4. **Undo of Add photos:** the added photos leave the job. Their files stay until retention, as Remove's do.
5. **Layer buttons on a photo whose overlap ends after a move:** the buttons follow the plan, enabled only while it overlaps.

### Task 2: the step store

**Files:**
- Create: `src/screens/adjust/history.ts` and `history.test.ts`
- Modify: `src/app.tsx`, where `forgetClipsExcept` is called

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `recordStep(jobId, before: JobEdits, beforeRev: number, afterRev: number): void`. It starts afresh when `beforeRev` is not the stored revision, and keeps at most 50 steps.
  - `stepsFor(jobId, rev): { canUndo: boolean; canRedo: boolean }`. Both are false when `rev` is not the stored revision.
  - `takeUndo(jobId, current: JobEdits): JobEdits | null` and `takeRedo(jobId, current: JobEdits): JobEdits | null`. Each moves `current` onto the other list.
  - `settle(jobId, rev)`: the revision after an Undo or Redo is saved.
  - `dropStep(jobId, kind: 'undo' | 'redo', edits)`: puts a step back when its save failed.
  - `forgetStepsExcept(jobIds: string[])`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** for each rule above: push, undo, redo, a new step clears redo, the 51st step drops the oldest, a revision mismatch gives nothing, and forget.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run** them. Expected: FAIL (module missing).
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement.** Use a module-level `Map`, as `clipboard.ts` does. Call it from `app.tsx`'s active-jobs effect.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run** them. Expected: PASS.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit.**
