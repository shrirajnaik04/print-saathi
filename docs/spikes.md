# Phase 0 spikes — go/no-go results

**Result values:**
- **PASS:** proven, with a link to the evidence.
- **PASS (emulator):** proven on emulators; the real-device part is still pending.
- **FAIL:** disproved.
- **BLOCKED (user):** needs the owner's phone, the shopkeeper's phone, the Brother printer or the shopkeeper in person; steps in `docs/spikes/USER-CHECKLIST.md`.
- **NOT RUN.**

Every PASS names the weakest point of its evidence.

**Evidence roots:** `docs/evidence/phase-0/2026-10-02/` (E1) and `docs/evidence/phase-0/2026-10-03/` (E2).

## Go/no-go

- **No spike disproved the R2 architecture.**
- **One bounded R2 amendment is needed for HEIC.** It is the R2 fallback "a normal separate process", applied to HEIF only, and needs the owner's choice in ADR-003.
- **One Phase 2 requirement was added:** the progressive-JPEG budget check.
- **Open user-only items** (§A–F) do not block Phase 1 (design system and shell), but they **do block Milestone A** (real printing). See `docs/qa/phase-0.md`.
